Quick reminder

An hour of staff time has a value even when nobody pays for it. Cost of a routine = minutes ÷ 60 × hourly value × runs per month; automate while MB ≥ MC; a sunk cost changes no decision; break-even hours = price ÷ hourly value; payback = set-up ÷ monthly net saving. Every formula reads a column of boreal_routines_2026.csv or one of the three given numbers, and becomes one EconEngine method (Brick 3).
