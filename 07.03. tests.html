Quiz — 30 questions

Formative self-assessment, not summative: this quiz is not graded and does not count toward your final grade. Questions 1–20 have one answer (4 options); questions 21–30 have several (6 options; the number expected is in round brackets). Academic integrity — Legal Notice §18.

Question 1 · Lesson 7.1
Five runs give Sxy = 20 and Sxx = 10 (x: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4; y: 6, 10, 11, 14, 14). What is the slope b?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The slope is a ratio of two of the three totals; Syy is not in it.
Question 2 · Lesson 7.1
With b = 2.0, x̄ = 2 and ȳ = 11, what is the intercept a of the least-squares line?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The least-squares line always passes through the point of the two means.
Question 3 · Lesson 7.1
For the 55 manual e-mail runs, r = +0.215. Which statement is correct?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The share of variation is not r itself; square it.
Question 4 · Lesson 7.1
The stand-up line ŷ = 6.66 + 2.21x was fitted on runs with 0 to 4 interruptions. A colleague asks for the forecast at 10 interruptions. What should the team answer?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Check where 10 lies compared with the smallest and largest x in the data.
Question 5 · Lesson 7.2
The first pattern written on the board, r"log\s+(\d+)\s*min\s+(\w+)" (no flag), is tested on three phrasings. Which one does it fail to match?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Look at the letter case, the spelling of the unit and the extra word.
Question 6 · Lesson 7.2
What does Agent.handle() do when the provider returns Intent("unknown", {"text": "book a flight to Paris"})?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The tools dictionary is read with .get(), not with square brackets.
Question 7 · Lesson 7.2
Why does Intent declare args: dict = field(default_factory=dict) instead of args: dict = {}?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Remember the mutable-default smell of Class 5.
Question 8 · Lesson 7.2
Which runs-table row does the request "log 0 min scheduling" produce?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The pattern matches \d+ — does 0 match? Then where is the 1–480 check?
Question 9 · Lesson 7.3
Which sentence is the decision of the e-mail recommendation?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: A decision tells the owner what to do and starts with an action verb.
Question 10 · Lesson 7.3
A rehearsal measures 35, 80, 150, 55 and 25 seconds against the plan 30, 60, 120, 60, 30. Which segment should the team trim first?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Compute measured minus planned for each segment.
Question 11 · Lesson 7.3
During the Alpha demo the program stops with a traceback. What is the best reaction?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The slot is five minutes and the rest of the run of show still has value.
Question 12 · Lesson 7.3
Which comment is a usable wish?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Could the team act on it tonight, knowing exactly what to change?
Question 13 · Lesson 7.1
For the stand-up line ŷ = 6.660 + 2.211x, what is the forecast for 2 interruptions?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Replace x by 2 in a + b·x.
Question 14 · Lesson 7.1
A run with 3 interruptions took 15 minutes. With ŷ = 6.660 + 2.211x, what is its residual?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Residual = observed − fitted.
Question 15 · Lesson 7.1
Why does a = 6.66 minutes have a practical meaning for stand-up notes?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: The intercept is a forecast at x = 0.
Question 16 · Lesson 7.2
Which piece of the OfflineProvider pattern lets "45min" (no space) match?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Which quantifier accepts zero occurrences?
Question 17 · Lesson 7.2
Requests 1–6 of Example 7.2 produce how many runs rows with ok = 0?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Look for the refused log and the request without a tool.
Question 18 · Lesson 7.2
Which class does the Agent need to change when a SummarizeTool is added?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Polymorphism: handle() calls tool.run() whatever the tool.
Question 19 · Lesson 7.3
Which change of the pilot group is linked to the assistant once the comparison group is taken into account?MCQ (multiple-choice question)
Hint: Subtract the comparison group's change from the pilot group's change.
