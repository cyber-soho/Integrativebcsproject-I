How to study with this hub

  1. Read the class hand-out once, lesson by lesson.
  2. Go through the flash cards of one lesson. Say your answer aloud before you flip the card, then mark it I knew it or Review again.
  3. Take the quiz. Use the hint only after a first attempt; read the explanation of every option after checking.
  4. On the results page, open the questions you missed, then retry only those.
  5. Before Brick 4, redo the four demos of the hand-out and compare their numbers with yours.
